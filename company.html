About Firstway International

OVERVIEW

A focused freight forwarder, built for clarity

Firstway International Co., Ltd. is a Seoul-based freight forwarding company organizing ocean, Eurasian rail, and trucking shipments for importers and exporters. We keep our operation lean by design — every shipment is handled directly by people who know the routing, the paperwork, and the partners involved, rather than passed through layers of coordination.

Our goal is simple: make international shipping predictable. That means clear quotes, realistic transit times, and a single point of contact who can answer questions before they become problems.
