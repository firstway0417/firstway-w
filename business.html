Our Services

SEAWAY

Ocean Freight

We arrange FCL and LCL ocean shipments for import and export cargo, coordinating directly with carriers and overseas agents to keep bookings, documentation, and customs clearance moving on schedule.

  • FCL / LCL booking and space allocation
  • Export & import documentation
  • Customs clearance coordination
  • Cargo insurance arrangement
RAILWAY

Eurasian Rail Freight

Our rail freight service connects Korea to Europe and Central Asia via the China Trans-continental Railway (TCR), Trans-Siberian Railway (TSR), and Trans-Mongolian Railway (TMGR) — a reliable middle ground between ocean and air, with transit times that beat sea freight at a fraction of air freight cost.

  • TCR / TSR / TMGR corridor routing
  • Block train & LCL rail options
  • Cross-border customs coordination
  • Door-to-door arrangement on request
TRUCKING

Domestic & Cross-border Trucking

We coordinate road transport within Korea and across borders, linking seamlessly with our ocean and rail services for true door-to-door delivery.

  • Port & terminal drayage
  • Domestic door-to-door delivery
  • Cross-border trucking coordination
  • Flexible scheduling for time-sensitive cargo
